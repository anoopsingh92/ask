03 / how I workA clear, simple way of working.

Good websites come from clear steps. This is how a typical project runs when we work together.

  1. 01

    Plan first

    We agree on goals, pages and deadlines before any code is written. I put the plan in writing, so everyone knows what to expect.

    You get: A written plan

  2. 02

    Build in small steps

    I share work in small parts that you can test. You see progress early and can ask for changes before they cost much.

    You get: Early previews

  3. 03

    Keep it fast

    Pages should load quickly on slow phones. I compress images, trim extra code and measure the result with Lighthouse.

    You get: Quick, light pages

  4. 04

    Test and hand over

    Before launch I check speed, accessibility and small screens. I leave clear notes so your team can keep going.

    You get: Clear handover notes
