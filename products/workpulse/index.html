WorkPulse SaaS.

One Pulse. One Platform. Better Work. An AI-powered workplace and project management platform by NextOpcode.

Details

WorkPulse helps organizations streamline operations, improve collaboration and work smarter by keeping people management and project delivery in a single connected platform, with integrations for the engineering tools teams already use. It brings HR, employee management, projects, tasks, time tracking, recruitment, payroll and engineering workflows together in one connected platform.

Key Features

Project Management

  • All projects and a personal “My Projects” view of assigned work
  • Task Management with an interactive Kanban board
  • Time Tracking with time logs and built-in task timer
  • Project Worklogs and sprint velocity reports
  • Sprint reports

Git & Repositories

  • Repositories overview
  • Issue tracking and pull request management
  • Commit history and inline code discussions
  • Real-time team engineering activity feed

WorkPulse AI

  • Built-in AI assistant, labelled Gemini AI in the navigation menu
  • AI-powered work log summarization, automated status reports, and contextual task generation

People & HR

  • Employee directories and personnel profiles
  • Attendance tracking and automated shift rosters
  • Shifts management
  • Leave management with multi-level approval workflows
  • Holidays holidays and official document repository
  • End-to-end talent recruitment pipeline
  • Documents vault for secure file storage

Payroll & Reporting

  • Payslip & payroll payslips and payroll processing
  • Reports & analytics operational reports and workplace analytics

Account & Administration

  • Dashboard Executive dashboard overview
  • My profile Personal profiles and custom preferences
  • System settings configurations and granular role-based permissions (RBAC)
