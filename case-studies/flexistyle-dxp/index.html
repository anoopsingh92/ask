Architecture Breakdown

Content editors maintain full access to Drupal's authoring, media management, and workflow governance, while the consumer-facing frontend is detached into a reactive Next.js serverless application.

  • Incremental Static Regeneration (ISR): Cached edge content served via CDN, rebuilding only invalidated paths when editorial revisions are published.
  • Pre-Configured Schema.org Markup: Automated structured data generation for products, breadcrumbs, and articles to maximize organic search indexing.
  • Sub-second TTFB: Edge caching paired with Varnish reverse proxying to support heavy concurrent traffic spikes.
