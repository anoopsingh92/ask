Platform Scope & Architecture

WorkPulse was created to eliminate fragmented tooling across engineering teams. By connecting time-tracking, task sprints, and developer attendance under one single dashboard, management teams gain immediate operational clarity without manual reporting overhead.

  • Real-time State Synchronization: Reactive UI updating team schedules and sprint workloads dynamically.
  • Granular Role-Based Permissions: Multi-tiered RBAC enforcing strict separation between managers, team leads, and external contractors.
  • Modular Microservices: Decoupled backend architecture providing resilient REST and GraphQL APIs for rapid client integrations.
